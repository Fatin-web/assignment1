Here are the steps I plan to take to achieve my dream of becoming a Graphic Designer:

  1. Graduate high school with strong art and tech skills
  2. Enroll in a Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design or related field
  3. Complete internships to gain real-world experience
  4. Master design tools like Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and Figma
  5. Build a strong online portfolio
  6. Network with professionals in the design community
  7. Apply for jobs at agencies or start freelancing
